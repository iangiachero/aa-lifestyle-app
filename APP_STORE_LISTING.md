## App name
All Access Lifestyle

## Description

Organize your life, all in one place.

All Access Lifestyle is an all in one organization app designed to simplify your everyday routine. Manage your schedule, tasks, meals, workouts, school, home, shopping, notes, and more from one streamlined space.

Everything you need in one place:

* Calendar: Manage your schedule with daily, weekly, and monthly views.
* Tasks & Checklists: Organize priorities and create personalized checklists for everyday life.
* Meal Planning & Recipes: Browse curated recipes or create your own, then add recipes directly to your meal plan and grocery list for easier planning.
* Grocery Lists: Keep everything you need for your meals and shopping organized in one place.
* Workouts: Access curated male and female workout sections or create custom workouts based on your goals.
* Lifestyle Routines: Use curated routines or create your own to fit your lifestyle.
* Student Section: Organize classes, schedules, academic responsibilities, and stay on top of upcoming work with a dedicated assignment tracker.
* Home Organization: Stay on top of your home with curated and custom organization checklists.
* Notes: Save important notes, ideas, reminders, and information.
* Shop: Save products, add shopping links, and keep track of items you want to purchase.
* Password Vault: Keep important login information organized in one dedicated space.
* Light & Dark Mode: Personalize the app to match your preferred look.

Whether you're managing school, work, fitness, meals, shopping, or home life, All Access Lifestyle helps you keep everything together and stay organized with ease.

Your life. Your routine. All Access.
